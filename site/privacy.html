Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This website

In short: this site sets no cookies, runs no tracking or analytics, and has no form that sends data anywhere. We don’t know who you are and we aren’t trying to find out.

Scope

This policy covers the OmniCore Lab website. The privacy principles behind PalmCore are described further down this page. The product will have its own privacy policy when it is released.

What we don’t do

  • We set no cookies and don’t use your browser’s local storage.
  • We run no analytics, advertising, social widgets or trackers of any kind.
  • There are no forms, sign-ups or logins. The site itself never receives anything you type.

What still happens

Hosting logs

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. As with any website, when your browser connects, Cloudflare sees your IP address and basic browser information, and may keep connection logs under its own privacy policy. We have not turned on Cloudflare’s analytics, and we don’t use those logs to identify or follow individual visitors.

Fonts

The typefaces on these pages are served by Google Fonts. Loading them means your browser contacts Google’s servers, so Google sees your IP address. We use no other Google service on this site.

When you email us

We receive your address and whatever you write. We use them to reply and to deal with the matter you wrote about, and for nothing else. We don’t add your address to any list or give it to third parties. Mail sent to contact@omnicore-lab.dev is forwarded by Cloudflare to the team’s mailbox and stored with that mailbox’s email provider.

You can ask us at any time to delete our correspondence with you.

Privacy principles for PalmCore

PalmCore has not been released to the public. It can’t be downloaded from this site. These are the principles we work to while building it:

  • Personal data is handled on the phone first.
  • Whatever doesn’t need to leave the phone stays on it.
  • The product remembers what a person has told it so that they don’t have to repeat themselves, and that memory isn’t used for anything else.

These are principles, not a full policy. When PalmCore is released it will have its own privacy policy, stating what data is collected, where it is kept, for how long, and what choices people have.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will be updated.
