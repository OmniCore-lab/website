PalmCore

An AI companion that lives in the phone, made for people who aren’t at home with smartphones.

What is built

  • A companion you talk to or type to, with voice in and out
  • Step-by-step guidance on top of whatever app you are in
  • Doing a task for you on the phone, one visible step at a time, with a confirmation before anything irreversible
  • A separate app for family: pairing by QR code, end-to-end encrypted messages, and screen sharing that starts only when the person agrees
  • Memory of what you have told it, kept on the phone
  • Masking of personal information on the phone before text goes to a cloud model

Who it’s for

The people nobody had in mind when the phone was designed

A modern phone assumes you already know a great deal: that three stacked lines can be tapped, that pulling the screen down refreshes it, which pop-up needs an answer and which one to ignore. Nobody teaches these things. You pick them up over years of use.

If you didn’t grow up with one, any step can be the one that stops you, and often there is no one around when it does. Many older adults are in exactly this position. Booking a clinic visit, paying a bill, keeping in touch with family: one by one these moved into the phone, and nobody asked them first.

We don’t think of them as people who need looking after. They have handled far more complicated things than a phone. What they want is to get their own things done without having to ask someone each time. That is what PalmCore is for.

Three commitments

PalmCore rests on three things

01It sits beside you

However clear the instructions are, once you’ve read them you are back in front of the screen by yourself. PalmCore stays. When you set out to do something, it goes through it with you one step at a time, at your pace.

The picture we keep in mind is a patient person in the next chair. They don’t hurry you, and their tone doesn’t change the second time you ask.

02It never leaves you stranded

When you get stuck, most software shows you the same hint again. PalmCore treats that moment as its cue: someone steps in, and family can help from a distance when they can’t be in the room.

The standard we hold ourselves to is that at no step should a person be left staring at the screen with no idea what to do.

03It remembers you

The tiring part of asking for help is often having to explain everything from the beginning. PalmCore remembers what you’ve told it, so next time you don’t have to.

Remembering someone is a responsibility. That is why this commitment can’t be discussed apart from our position on privacy, below.

Privacy

Personal data stays on the phone first

A companion that helps you use your phone will come across very private things. Our position: personal data is handled on the phone first, and leaves it only when it has to.

This is where the design starts. It is not a setting added at the end. It makes a number of features harder to build, and we think that cost belongs to us, not to the people using the product.

When PalmCore is released it will come with its own privacy policy, spelling out what is collected, where it is kept and for how long.

Design position

A few things we decided not to do

  • No “senior mode”Enlarging the text and removing features tells a person “this is all you get.” We want to help people use the things they wanted to use in the first place.
  • No scoresNobody is here to sit an exam. Learning quickly or slowly should never turn into a number on the screen.
  • No hurryingBeing willing to wait is the most basic thing this product has to be able to do.

Where it stands

Built · in tuning

Built, in tuning, not yet released

The main features are built and running on real phones. What we are doing now is tuning them. There is no public download yet. We keep a dated progress log, and part of the work is on GitHub.
