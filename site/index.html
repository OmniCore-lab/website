OmniCore Lab · Taiwan

We’re building an AI companion for people the smartphone was never designed for.

Phones assume everyone already knows how they work. People who don’t are handed a help article, a tutorial video, or a relative saying “it’s easy.” We think the design should change, not the person.

OmniCore Lab is a small R&D team in Taiwan. Right now we are mainly working on one product: PalmCore.

Product

PalmCore: an AI companion that lives in the phone

It is made for people who aren’t at home with smartphones, older adults in particular. We aren’t trying to add one more app to learn. We want something in the phone that stays with you until the thing you came to do is done.

Sits beside you
It goes through each step with you. It doesn’t hand you instructions and leave.
Never leaves you stranded
When you’re stuck, someone steps in. Family can help from a distance.
Remembers you
It keeps what you’ve told it, so you don’t start over every time.

The thinking behind PalmCore, and where it stands

Principles

Four things we decide by

  1. Change the design, not the person

    When someone struggles with a phone, the usual verdict is that they “can’t use it.” We don’t see it that way. Someone who has worked for decades and raised a family can learn anything reasonable. If they can’t learn it, it isn’t reasonable. So we don’t build a “simplified mode,” we don’t treat bigger text as a solution, and we don’t grade people.

  2. Being there matters more than explaining well

    Even the best instructions leave a person alone with the screen. We would rather spend our effort on the moment itself: the step someone is on, at the pace they are going.

  3. Data stays close to the person

    Personal data is handled on the phone first, and leaves it only when it has to. That makes a lot of things harder to build. We accept that.

  4. We don’t claim what we haven’t measured

    If we haven’t tested something, we say we haven’t. The numbers on this site come with how they were measured and what they don’t show.

Status

Built · in tuning

The main features are built. We are tuning.

PalmCore runs on real phones today and does what this site describes. The work now is tuning: accuracy, speed on older phones, and the rough edges you only find by using it. See what exists today and the dated progress log.

It has not been released to the public yet.
